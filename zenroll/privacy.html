Zenroll Privacy Policy

Effective 4 November 2026

Zenroll is a photo and storage clean-up app for iPhone. It is built so that your photos never leave your device.

What Zenroll does with your photos

With your permission, Zenroll reads your photo library on the device to find screenshots, large videos, blurry photos, exact duplicates and visually similar photos. All analysis, including image similarity, runs entirely on your iPhone using Apple's on-device frameworks. No photo, thumbnail, feature print or metadata is uploaded anywhere.

Zenroll only deletes items after you confirm the system deletion prompt. Deleted items go to the Photos app's Recently Deleted album, where iOS keeps them for up to 30 days.

Data we collect

None. Zenroll has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting SDK. The app does not contain networking code. Its App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected".

Data stored on your device

Zenroll keeps a small local database of scan results (per-photo similarity signatures, sharpness scores and your "protect" / "dismiss" decisions) so that later scans are faster, plus your settings and the list of items waiting in Zenroll's own trash. This data stays in the app's sandbox and is removed when you delete the app.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. Zenroll receives only the entitlement status from Apple's StoreKit framework; it never sees your payment details. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

Diagnostics you choose to share

Settings › Export diagnostics creates a text log of scan events (counts, sizes, timings and error messages, never photo content) that you can send to us voluntarily through the share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you do so.

Children

Zenroll is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
