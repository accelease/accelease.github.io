What Zenroll is

Zenroll helps you find and remove photos and videos you may no longer need. It suggests items; you decide. Every deletion requires your confirmation in the iOS system prompt, and deleted items are kept by iOS in Recently Deleted for up to 30 days.

Your responsibility

Review suggestions before deleting. Zenroll's similarity, blur and screenshot detection are automated and can make mistakes. We are not liable for photos or videos you choose to delete. Keep a backup (for example iCloud Photos) of anything you cannot afford to lose.

Free plan and Zenroll Pro

The free plan lets you delete up to 500 MB per calendar month through Zenroll; exact duplicates are always free. Zenroll Pro removes that limit and is sold as an auto-renewing yearly subscription (with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers), a monthly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app in your local currency before you buy.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. You can manage or cancel in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple under App Store policy.
