Zenroll

Free up iPhone storage in one calm, guided session. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is deleted without your say-so.

🧭 One guided session

Zenroll scans your library on the device and builds a short plan: old screenshots, the biggest videos, look-alike bursts and blurry shots. Walk through it step by step and confirm once at the end.

🎯 Smart, not reckless

Similar photos are grouped and the sharpest shot is kept automatically. Exact duplicates are found byte for byte. Every number is the real file size, so you know exactly what you get back.

🔒 Safe by design

Everything stays in Zenroll's own holding area until you confirm, and iOS keeps deleted items in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Full on-device processing: no account, no analytics, no uploads.

Free and Pro
